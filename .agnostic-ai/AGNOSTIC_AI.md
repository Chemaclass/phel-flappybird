# phel-flappybird

Terminal Flappy Bird in Phel (compiles to PHP). Source in `src/`, tests in `tests/`.

- Install: `composer install`. Run: `composer dev`. Compile and play: `composer start`.
- Gate: `composer ci` (validate, format-check, lint, test, build).
- Layout, architecture and release: the architecture rule. Phel idioms: the phel-conventions rule.
